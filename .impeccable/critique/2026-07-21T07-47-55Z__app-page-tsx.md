## Overall Impression

The site feels calm, careful, and unusually responsible about medical claims. Its biggest opportunity is to become one confident, verifiable personal invitation instead of a long catalogue of services, packages, lists, and generated identity imagery. The current missing webhook and WhatsApp configuration turns the intended reassuring ending into a conversion and trust cliff.

## What’s Working

- The hero immediately communicates the niche, Den Bosch location, personal approach, and primary action.
- Medical-claim safety is strong: coaching is complementary, medication decisions remain with physicians, and Astrid’s result is explicitly not guaranteed for others.
- The health-check form is intentionally light: name, phone, and one three-option choice, with autocomplete, visible labels, submitting state, input preservation, and accessible status roles.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The primary conversion route is unavailable in the current project configuration

**Why it matters:** Every major CTA funnels to the health-check form. The project has no local/production environment file and the running shell has neither `GHL_WEBHOOK_URL` nor `NEXT_PUBLIC_WHATSAPP_NUMBER`; the route explicitly returns 503 without the webhook, while the WhatsApp component renders nothing without its number.

**Fix:** Configure and verify both integrations. Until they exist, show a visible clickable email fallback before submission rather than revealing plain email text only after failure. Validate phone structure rather than character count alone.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P1] Identity and credential presentation is too synthetic for a health-trust context

**Why it matters:** Generated assets are described as literal photographs of Astrid even though verified portrait assets exist. BLCN, Sonnevelt, LinkedIn, and media proof are mostly asserted rather than inspectable. Health-adjacent decisions demand authenticity.

**Fix:** Use client-approved photography for identity-bearing scenes. Link the BLCN membership, training, LinkedIn profile, and media evidence wherever verification is possible.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`

### [P1] The homepage behaves like a catalogue instead of one persuasive journey

**Why it matters:** Five services, four packages, long process lists, a nine-paragraph story, and seven FAQs delay the strongest proof and diffuse the health-check decision.

**Fix:** Preserve the mandated SEO copy but reorder the experience to empathy → verified proof → approach → simple offer → form. Use selective progressive disclosure and visual grouping for lower-priority details.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P2] The visual language relies on AI-era wellness defaults

**Why it matters:** Warm cream, sage, terracotta, Outfit, oversized rounding, broad shadows, and repeated bordered cards do not yet feel owned by Astrid. The main button combines a border with a 28px shadow, matching the ghost-card tell.

**Fix:** Anchor the system in a more specific physical reference: practical Dutch coaching with Scandinavian outdoor clarity. Reduce card usage, cap card radii at 12–16px, flatten decorative shadows, and strengthen the typography without changing the approved copy.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable bolder`

### [P2] Accessibility and mobile details underserve older or pain-affected visitors

**Why it matters:** Placeholder contrast is approximately 3.26:1; language controls are about 27×30px and the menu button 42×42px; the mobile CTA anchor lands above a 200px image instead of at the form; reveal content depends on JavaScript; the mobile menu lacks Escape/focus handling.

**Fix:** Meet 4.5:1 placeholder contrast, use at least 44×44px touch targets, move the anchor to the form heading, manage focus and Escape behavior, and make reveal motion progressive enhancement.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable audit`
